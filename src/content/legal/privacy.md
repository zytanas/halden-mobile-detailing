## Who we are

{name} Ltd ("{shortName}", "we", "us") provides mobile car detailing across {region}. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website and our bookings. You can contact us about privacy at {email} or {phone}.

## What we collect

- Contact details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and postcode
- Booking details, such as your vehicle size, chosen package, add-ons, address and appointment time
- Messages you send us by phone, email or social media
- Basic technical information needed to load the website, such as your IP address, which is processed by our hosting and map providers

## How we use it

We use your information to check we cover your area, give you a price, arrange and carry out your booking, contact you about your appointment, handle payments and refunds, run the {club} and referral offers, and reply to your questions. We'll only send marketing emails if you've joined the {club} or asked us to, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

## Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law we rely on: contract (to provide the service you've booked), legitimate interests (to run and improve our business and reply to enquiries), consent (for {club} emails, which you can withdraw at any time) and legal obligation (for example, keeping financial records).

## Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with services that help us run the business:

- Cal.com, which handles online bookings
- Cloudflare, which hosts this website
- CARTO and OpenStreetMap, which provide the map tiles on our service area map (your browser requests the map images directly from them)
- Our payment provider, when you pay by card
- Our insurer or professional advisers, if needed for a claim or legal matter

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, they use appropriate safeguards required by UK law.

## Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and our fonts are hosted on our own site. Our booking provider may use essential cookies when you open the booking page; please see their own policy.

## How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for 6 years for accounting purposes. Enquiries that don't lead to a booking are deleted after 12 months. {club} details are kept until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, to ask us to transfer it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email {email}. We'll reply within one month.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
